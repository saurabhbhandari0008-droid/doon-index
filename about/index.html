About Doon Dekho

A Dehradun guide made by someone who lives here

Doon Dekho is an independent guide to Dehradun: where to eat, what to see, where to shop and study, written from the city itself.

Who writes it

I'm Saurabh. I was born in Dehradun, went to college, moved to Gurgaon for a while and came back, because nowhere else had the bakeries, the evening walks up Rajpur Road or the quiet of the valley after rain. Doon Dekho is the guide I wanted when friends asked me where to go.

How places get on the site

  • Verified places (84 so far) have been checked by us. They get their own page with an area, budget, best time to go and a local tip.
  • Unverified listings come from public sources and are marked as such. We add details only after checking them, and owners can claim theirs.
  • Listings from public sources are well-known public places we haven't visited yet. Their pages say so and link the sources for timings and fees.
  • Featured labels mark places with a business relationship with Doon Dekho. It is always shown.

Photos
