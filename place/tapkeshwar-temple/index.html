Quick answers

Where is Tapkeshwar Temple in Dehradun?

Tapkeshwar Temple (also called Tapkeshwar Mahadev) is in Garhi Cantt, Dehradun. Open it in Google Maps for directions from where you are.

What are the timings of Tapkeshwar Temple?

About 6 am to 7 pm (open day and night in Shravan). Timings can change, so check before you go.

When is the best time to visit Tapkeshwar Temple?

Morning.

Any tips before going to Tapkeshwar Temple?

Visit on a weekday to avoid queues.
