Indian Military Academy

Also called IMA, Chetwode Hall

The academy that trains officers of the Indian Army, founded in 1932 on the former Railway College campus. Its main building, Chetwode Hall, is named after Field Marshal Sir Philip Chetwode, who opened the academy. The campus has a museum and a war memorial, but entry needs special permission; the public is let in on GC day, the cadets' graduation day.
