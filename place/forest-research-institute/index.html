Forest Research Institute

Also called FRI

A grand colonial-era building set in a huge forest campus, with museums on forestry and wood. One of the city’s most photographed landmarks.

Quick answers

Where is Forest Research Institute in Dehradun?

Forest Research Institute (also called FRI) is in Chakrata Road, Dehradun, about 7 km from the Clock Tower. Open it in Google Maps for directions from where you are.

What are the timings of Forest Research Institute?

Two visitor sessions: 10 am to 1:30 pm and 1:30 to 5 pm. Timings can change, so check before you go.

Is there an entry fee at Forest Research Institute?

₹100 per Indian visitor per session; the museums are ₹150 extra for Indian adults (rates from 25 May 2026).

When is the best time to visit Forest Research Institute?

Morning.

Any tips before going to Forest Research Institute?

Give yourself two hours. The campus is bigger than it looks.
