Quick answers

Where is Dehradun Zoo in Dehradun?

Dehradun Zoo (also called Malsi Deer Park) is in Malsi, Mussoorie Road, Dehradun, about 10 km from the city, on the Mussoorie road. Open it in Google Maps for directions from where you are.

What are the timings of Dehradun Zoo?

About 10 am to 5 pm, closed on Mondays. Timings can change, so check before you go.

When is the best time to visit Dehradun Zoo?

Weekday morning.

Any tips before going to Dehradun Zoo?

Check opening days before you go; most zoos close one day a week.
