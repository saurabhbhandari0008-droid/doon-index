Ramakrishna Ashrama

Also called Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama

A Ramakrishna Mission ashrama at Kishanpur, started as a retreat in 1916 and part of the Belur Math order since 1934. Swami Vivekananda visited Dehradun in 1890 and 1897. The ashrama also runs the Vivekananda Netralaya eye hospital.
