Quick answers

Where is Robber’s Cave in Dehradun?

Robber’s Cave (also called Guchhupani or Gucchupani) is in Guchhupani, Dehradun, about 8 km from the city. Open it in Google Maps for directions from where you are.

What are the timings of Robber’s Cave?

About 7 am to 6 pm. Timings can change, so check before you go.

When is the best time to visit Robber’s Cave?

8 am.

Any tips before going to Robber’s Cave?

Arrive early. The tea stalls at the entrance open around nine.
