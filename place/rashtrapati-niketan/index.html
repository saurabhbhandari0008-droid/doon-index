Rashtrapati Niketan

Also called President's Retreat, Rashtrapati Ashiana

The President's 21-acre retreat on Rajpur Road, used as a summer camp for the Governor General's Bodyguard from 1838 and a Presidential Retreat since 1976. Open to the public since July 2025, with guided tours of the heritage building, the stables and horses of the President's Bodyguard, lily and rockery ponds, a rose garden, a cafeteria and a souvenir shop.
