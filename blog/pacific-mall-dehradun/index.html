Pacific Mall Dehradun: Stores, PVR, Funky Island and Parking

Pacific Mall on Rajpur Road, Jakhan: the big stores, the five-screen PVR, the Funky Island play centre, the food court and parking.

Pacific Mall is on Rajpur Road at Jakhan, and for most people on the north side of Dehradun it's the nearest big mall. It's Pacific Group's first mall in the city; the much larger Mall of Dehradun on Haridwar Road is its second. Pacific Mall has around 1.2 lakh square feet of space and more than 120 stores.

Stores

The big names are Shoppers Stop and Lifestyle, with brands like Levi's, Nike and Puma alongside. Stores change from time to time, so check a brand's store locator before making a special trip.

Cinema

The PVR has five screens. It's the most convenient multiplex for anyone living on Rajpur Road, Jakhan, Kishanpur or the Mussoorie side.

Funky Island, for kids

Funky Island is an indoor family entertainment centre in the mall. It has a 7D experience, bumper cars, 9D virtual reality rides, arcade games, a soft play area and party zones. Tickets start at ₹750, entry is for ages 4 and above, and it's open from about 11 am to 10:30 pm. Prices change, so check before you go.

Food

There's a large food court with the usual chains. Rajpur Road also has some of the city's best-known cafés within a short drive, so if you want a proper sit-down meal, you have options outside the mall too.

Parking and timings

The mall has parking for cars and two-wheelers. Most Dehradun malls are open from around 10 or 11 am to 10 pm.

Pacific Mall vs Mall of Dehradun

Pacific Mall is closer for most of the city and easier for a quick trip or a film. Mall of Dehradun is bigger, with more international brands, Croma and a hotel on site, but it's on Haridwar Road on the far side of town.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Pacific Mall in Dehradun?

On Rajpur Road at Jakhan.

How many screens does the PVR at Pacific Mall have?

Five.

Is there a kids' play area at Pacific Mall?

Yes. Funky Island has bumper cars, VR rides, arcade games and a soft play area, for ages 4 and above.

Does Pacific Mall have parking?

Yes, for cars and two-wheelers.
