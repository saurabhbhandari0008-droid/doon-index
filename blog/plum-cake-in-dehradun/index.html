Plum Cake in Dehradun: Where to Buy Christmas Cake This Year

Rajpur Road and Paltan Bazaar have been selling plum cake since the 1950s. Which bakeries to try, what to ask at the counter, and how to keep a cake until New Year.

Plum cake in Dehradun is mostly a Rajpur Road and Paltan Bazaar thing. The bakeries that sell it are the old family ones near the Clock Tower, and in December people buy from them for their own tables and for relatives in other cities. This guide covers where to buy, what to ask before you pay, and how to keep a cake fresh from Christmas to New Year.

We haven't done a side-by-side tasting yet, so this isn't a ranking. The names below come from bakery websites, travel writers and review listings, and we say where each detail comes from. Hours in particular differ between listings, so call before you make a special trip.

What "plum cake" means here

There are no plums in it. In India, plum cake is the dark, dense Christmas cake made with raisins, dried fruit, candied peel and spices, and many bakeries sell a lighter fruit cake alongside it. Some versions are soaked in rum or brandy and some aren't. If that matters to you, ask at the counter, because the label on a wrapped slab won't always tell you.

The fruit for a Christmas cake is often soaked weeks in advance, at a "cake mixing" where everyone takes a turn stirring. Hotels and catering schools hold these in late November and early December. In Dehradun, the Uttaranchal School of Hotel and Hospitality Management held one on 8 December 2025 and called it the first step towards the traditional Christmas fruitcake.

Where to buy plum cake in Dehradun

Ellora's, Rajpur Road

The name most people give first. Krishan Lal Gulati, an ex-army man, started Ellora's on Rajpur Road in 1953, and the family still runs it. The bakery's own website lists plum cakes among its most popular items, along with rusks and stick jaws. Outlook Traveller, writing in 2023, named the plum cake and the fruit cake as the two specialities not to miss.

There are two Ellora's shops a short walk apart on lower Rajpur Road, and people mix them up. Ellora's Melting Moments is at 29 Rajpur Road, opposite St. Joseph's Academy. The other, listed as Ellora's at 25 Rajpur Road, is opposite Astley Hall. Either is the first stop if you're walking up from the Clock Tower.

Ellora's website gives the Rajpur Road hours as 8 am to 10 pm every day, but the district.in listing says 8 am to 5 pm. Go in the morning and you won't have to find out which is right.

Sunrise Bakers, Paltan Bazaar

Sunrise Bakers is at 20 Ghosi Gali, near Darshan Lal Chowk in Paltan Bazaar. Outlook Traveller says Harnam Singh Jolly started it in 1956, though one older city guide gives 1948. It's best known for butter pista biscuits and rusks, and the DoonCircle guide lists plum cake among its specialities. A Tripadvisor reviewer in 2017 called the plum cake, rusk and pistachio biscuits "too good" and the prices "nominal". That review is old, so treat it as a reason to go and look, not as a promise.

The lanes of Paltan Bazaar are too narrow to drive into comfortably. Park at the edge of the market and walk in.

Ellora's outside the city

If you're flying out, Ellora's has an outlet at Jolly Grant Airport, which its website lists as open 6 am to 7 pm. In March 2026 it also opened a shop on Mall Road in Mussoorie, at Clarence House. That's useful if you're spending Christmas up the hill, though the items listed in a March 2026 report on the opening don't include plum cake, so ask first.

Local tip · The popular plum cakes can sell out close to the 25th. Buy or order a few days early, especially if you need several for gifts.

Ordering plum cake online

Ellora's website has separate ordering pages for delivery inside Dehradun and for delivery across India, and it gives a local number for Zomato and Swiggy orders. It's the one old bakery we found with its own national delivery, so it's the easiest way to send Dehradun plum cake to family outside Uttarakhand. Check the site in December for what's in stock, because the product list we saw had rusks, toffees, biscuits, cream rolls and pastries but no cake section.

What to ask at the counter

  • Does it have alcohol? Some Christmas cakes are soaked and some aren't.
  • Is there egg in it? Most traditional plum cake does. If you need eggless, ask whether they make one or take orders for it.
  • When was it baked? A cake from that morning and one from last week will both be fine, but you'll want to know for storage.
  • What weight are the slabs? Sizes vary, and you may want a small one to try before buying a box of them as gifts.

Keeping a plum cake until New Year

Plum cake keeps far better than a cream cake, because the dried fruit and sugar hold moisture. Keep it wrapped in its paper inside an airtight box, somewhere cool. You only need the fridge if the cake has a cream or icing layer. If you're carrying it on a train or flight, put it in your hand luggage so it doesn't get crushed.

A Christmas morning in the old town

If you want to make a morning of it, start with the plum cake run on Rajpur Road, since the old bakeries open early. St. Francis Church and St. John's Church have held special Christmas masses in past years. Where can I buy plum cake in Dehradun?

Ellora's on lower Rajpur Road and Sunrise Bakers in Ghosi Gali, Paltan Bazaar are the two old bakeries most linked with plum cake. Both are close to the Clock Tower.

Is Ellora's plum cake available online?

Ellora's website has ordering pages for Dehradun and for delivery across India, plus a number for Zomato and Swiggy orders. Check what's in stock in December.

Does plum cake have plums in it?

No. Indian plum cake is a dark fruit cake made with raisins, dried fruit, candied peel and spices. Some are soaked in rum or brandy.

How long does plum cake stay fresh?

Much longer than a cream cake. Keep it wrapped in an airtight box somewhere cool and it will be fine from Christmas to New Year. Ask the bakery for their own guidance on the cake you buy.

When should I buy Christmas cake in Dehradun?

A few days before 25 December. The old bakeries get busiest just before Christmas and the popular cakes can sell out.
