Malls in Dehradun: Shops, Cinemas, Food Courts and Parking

The four main malls in Dehradun, where each one is, which cinema it has, what's in the food court and how parking works.

Dehradun has four malls that most people actually go to. Two belong to the same company, and they are on opposite sides of the city. Which one you pick usually comes down to where you live and which cinema is showing the film you want.

Pacific Mall, Rajpur Road

Pacific Mall in Jakhan, on Rajpur Road, is the Pacific Group's first mall in Dehradun. It has a five-screen PVR, a large food court and big stores such as Shoppers Stop and Lifestyle, along with brands like Levi's and Nike. There's parking for cars and two-wheelers in the building.

It's busiest on weekend evenings. If you're coming from the Mussoorie side, it's the most convenient.

Mall of Dehradun, Haridwar Road

Mall of Dehradun is the newer and much bigger one, opened by the same Pacific Group in June 2024. It's at Mohkampur Khurd on Haridwar Road, on the southern side of the city. With over 10 lakh square feet of built-up space it's the largest mall in Uttarakhand. The PVR INOX multiplex is on the second floor. The food court gives space to Uttarakhand food, and there's a Lemon Tree hotel on the same premises.

If you live in Doiwala, Rishikesh Road or the Haridwar Road colonies, this is the closer mall. From Rajpur Road it's a long drive across town, so plan for the traffic.

Centrio Mall, Tagore Marg

Centrio is on Rabindranath Tagore Marg in Vijay Colony, near the Cantonment side of the city. The PVR is on the fourth floor, and the stores include Lifestyle, Westside and Adidas. The food court is called FoodRio.

Crossroads Mall, Old Survey Road

Crossroads, at 1 Old Survey Road in Karanpur, is compact. It has a multiplex, a food court, a gaming zone and a kids' play area, and valet parking. It's open roughly 11 am to 11 pm, which is a little later than the others.

Which mall should you go to?

For the biggest choice of shops, go to Mall of Dehradun or Pacific Mall. For a quick film in the city centre, Centrio or Crossroads. With small children, Crossroads has a play area and is easy to get around.

Timings and parking

Most malls in Dehradun open around 10 or 11 am and close around 10 or 11 pm. All four have parking on site.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the biggest mall in Dehradun?

Mall of Dehradun on Haridwar Road. It opened in June 2024 and is the largest mall in Uttarakhand.

Which malls in Dehradun have a PVR?

Pacific Mall on Rajpur Road, Mall of Dehradun on Haridwar Road and Centrio Mall on Tagore Marg all have PVR multiplexes.

What are mall timings in Dehradun?

Roughly 10 am to 10 pm. Crossroads Mall is open until about 11 pm.

Is there parking at Pacific Mall?

Yes, for cars and two-wheelers.
