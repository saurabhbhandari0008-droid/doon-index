Day school or boarding? Choosing a school in Dehradun

Dehradun has some of India’s best-known schools. A simple way to compare boards, campuses and costs.

Dehradun has some of India’s best-known boarding schools and many strong day schools. For parents, the choice can feel overwhelming. A few questions, asked in the right order, make it much simpler.

Start with the board

CBSE, ICSE/ISC and IB lead to different styles of teaching and assessment. Decide which suits your child before you compare campuses.

Then distance and boarding

A day school close to home means more time as a family. Boarding offers structure and independence. Be honest about which your child will thrive in.

Visit in term time. An empty campus tells you very little.

Ask about the full cost

Fee structures differ a lot. Ask for the full annual cost in writing, including boarding, uniforms, trips and activities.

Local tip · Talk to parents of current students. They will tell you what the brochure doesn’t.
