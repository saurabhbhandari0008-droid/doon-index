Pet Owner's Guide to Dehradun: Pet Shops, Vets, Groomers and Dog-Friendly Walks

Pet shops, vet clinics, groomers and kennels in Dehradun, all in one place, plus local tips for walking your dog and taking your pet up to the hills.

Dehradun is a good city to be a pet in. The weather is mild most of the year, there's green space almost everywhere, and the number of pet shops, vet clinics and groomers has grown quickly over the last few years. The problem is that the information is scattered across Google Maps, Instagram and the neighbourhood WhatsApp group.

So we've put it in one place.

Vet clinics

Save the number of a vet near your home before you need it. You don't want to be searching at 11 pm.

PetCuro Hospital & Surgery, Race Course: a pet hospital that does surgery as well as regular consultations.

  • Alpha-Vets Pet Clinic, near Balliwala Chowk: a clinic with its own pharmacy and pet supplies.
  • Pet Medics Veterinary Care & Diagnostic Centre, Kaulagarh Road: veterinary care and diagnostics.
  • PET+ Complete Veterinary Care, Race Course: veterinary care, including skin and dermatology.
  • Tip: ask your vet whether they handle emergencies, and what you should do after hours.

Pet shops and kennels

The Pet Nation, Patel Nagar: pet supplies, plus boarding and grooming.

Bark N Bite Pet Shop and Bark for Love, Chakrata Road (opposite The Doon School): two pet shops on the busy Chakrata Road stretch.

Pure and Sure Dog Kennel, Laxman Chowk.

Oh My Pets, Kanwali: a pet store with grooming, a pet bakery and dog clothes.

Pet grooming

  • Happy Tails Pet Grooming Salon, Kaulagarh Road.
  • Grooming Tales, Sahastradhara Road.
  • Several pet shops also do grooming, so ask when you visit.

Walking your dog in Dehradun

Early mornings are the best time, especially in summer, before the roads heat up.

Quiet residential roads in Dalanwala and upper Rajpur Road are pleasant for walks.

Carry water for your dog and clean up after them. It's the reason some neighbourhoods stay friendly to dog walkers.

Avoid busy markets like Paltan Bazaar. They're crowded and noisy, and most dogs hate it.

Taking your pet up to the hills

  • The roads to Mussoorie and beyond are steep and winding. If your pet gets car-sick, short breaks help.
  • Check that your hotel or homestay allows pets before you book, not when you arrive.
  • Carry vaccination records when you travel.

Choosing a pet boarding service

  • Visit before you book, and look at where the animals actually sleep.
  • Ask how often pets are walked and fed.
  • Ask for references from other owners.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find a 24-hour vet in Dehradun?

Emergency hours change, so call clinics directly and ask. Keep the number of the clinic nearest to you saved on your phone.

Are there pet boarding services in Dehradun?

Yes. Several pet shops and kennels in the city offer boarding. Visit before booking.

Is Dehradun a good city for dogs?

Yes. The mild climate and green spaces make it a pleasant city for dogs, especially in winter.
