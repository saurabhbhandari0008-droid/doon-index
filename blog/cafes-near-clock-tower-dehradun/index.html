Cafes Near Clock Tower and Paltan Bazaar, Dehradun

The old centre of Doon has more sweet shops than coffee shops. Where to sit down when you've been walking Paltan Bazaar all afternoon.

Cafes near Clock Tower in Dehradun are fewer than you'd expect. The city's café strip starts further up Rajpur Road, past Dilaram Chowk, and the streets around the tower belong to cloth shops, sweet shops, banks and the bakeries that have been here since the 1950s. Still, if you've spent an afternoon in Paltan Bazaar and need to sit down, there are a handful of places within a short walk.

We haven't reviewed these places ourselves yet. The hours and prices below come from their listings on EazyDiner and district.in, and listings in this part of town go out of date quickly. Check before you go, especially on Sundays and holidays.

The Clock Tower, briefly

Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower) stands where Rajpur Road, Chakrata Road and the Paltan Bazaar lanes meet. It's six-sided, with a clock on each face. Lala Sher Singh built it in memory of his father, Lala Balbir Singh, and Sarojini Naidu inaugurated it in 1953. Most people use it as the meeting point for a day in the old town, and every place below is described from there.

Cafes near Clock Tower Dehradun: where to sit

Just Cafe, Rajpur Road at the Clock Tower

The closest proper café to the tower. It's listed at 32 Rajpur Road, opposite the post office, and the menu covers coffee, desserts, pizza, pasta, momos and Chinese. Reviews keep coming back to the cheese garlic bread and the cappuccino. EazyDiner lists it as open 11 am to 11 pm, about ₹300 for two, with air conditioning and no parking of its own.

Barista, Rajpur Road

A Barista branch is listed at 15A Rajpur Road, in the same building as the English Book Depot, a little way up from the tower. It's the place to go if you want a predictable chain coffee and a quiet corner, or a coffee after browsing books. Its listing has very few ratings, so confirm it's open before making a special trip.

Paltan Cafe & Restaurant, Chakrata Road

Despite the name, this is more of a restaurant than a café, with Chinese and North Indian food. It's at 35 Chakrata Road, near the Clock Tower on the Connaught Place side. Listed hours are 10 am to 11 pm and it's about ₹500 for two. It has only a few ratings online, so we can't say much more about it yet.

Kwik Bite Centre, Gandhi Road

A snack place rather than somewhere to linger. Its listing puts it on Gandhi Road beside Allahabad Bank, at the Paltan Bazaar end, and the menu runs from pizza and momos to dosa. It's listed as open 10:30 am to 9:30 pm and about ₹300 for two. Good for a quick plate between shops.

Haldiram's, Chakrata Road

If you're with family and want something everyone will eat, the Haldiram's in City Centre Plaza near Prabhat Talkies on Chakrata Road is a safe choice. It's listed as open 9 am to 11 pm, about ₹400 for two, which makes it one of the later options here along with Just Cafe.

Kumar Vegetarian, Rajpur Road

For a full sit-down meal rather than coffee, Kumar Vegetarian at 15B Rajpur Road serves North and South Indian food. It opens at 11 am. It's the priciest place on this list, listed at around ₹1,000 for two.

The old bakeries near the Clock Tower

This part of town is better known for bakeries than cafés, and most of them are for buying, not sitting. They're worth knowing about because this is what people from Dehradun carry home.

  • Ellora's Melting Moments, 29 Rajpur Road. Founded in 1953 by Krishan Lal Gulati, and the first Ellora's you reach going from the Clock Tower up Rajpur Road. It's known for stick jaws, plum cake, pista biscuits and rusks, and also sells puffs, patties and cold coffee. district.in lists it as 8 am to 5 pm.
  • Sunrise Bakers, Ghosi Gali, Darshan Lal Chowk. Founded in 1956 by Harnam Singh Jolly. Known for butter pista biscuits and rusks.
  • Grand Bakers, 151/1 Paltan Bazaar. Known for cake rusk, saunf rusk and milk rusk.

Local tip · Ellora's is listed as closing at 5 pm, earlier than the cafés. If you want to carry rusks or plum cake home, go there first and do the bazaar afterwards.

Getting there and parking

Parking around the Clock Tower is hard, and the lanes of Paltan Bazaar are too narrow to drive into comfortably. Park on the edge of the market and walk. An underground car park for about 390 vehicles between Parade Ground and Gandhi Park has been proposed, but as of May 2026 it was still under review, so don't count on it yet.

Gandhi Park, next to the tower on Rajpur Road, is useful if you have children with you. It's free to enter and open from 5 to 9 am and 4 to 6:30 pm, so it fits an evening walk before or after a snack.

If you want a longer café afternoon

Walk or take an auto up Rajpur Road. The cafés become more frequent after Dilaram Chowk and cluster around Kishanpur. Our Rajpur Road café guide covers that stretch, and our city-wide café list covers Dalanwala and Chakrata Road.

Are there any cafes near Clock Tower in Dehradun?

Yes, a few. Just Cafe is on Rajpur Road opposite the post office at the Clock Tower, and a Barista branch is listed at 15A Rajpur Road. Most of Dehradun's cafés are further up Rajpur Road.

Where can I eat near Paltan Bazaar?

Kwik Bite Centre on Gandhi Road is at the Paltan Bazaar end for quick snacks. Haldiram's and Paltan Cafe & Restaurant are on Chakrata Road near the Clock Tower for a sit-down meal.

Which old bakeries are near the Clock Tower?

Ellora's Melting Moments on Rajpur Road, Sunrise Bakers at Darshan Lal Chowk and Grand Bakers in Paltan Bazaar. All three are known for rusks and biscuits.

Who built the Clock Tower in Dehradun?

Lala Sher Singh built it in memory of his father, Lala Balbir Singh. It was inaugurated by Sarojini Naidu in 1953.

Where should I park to visit Paltan Bazaar?

Park on the edge of the market and walk in. The lanes are narrow, and there is no large public car park at the Clock Tower yet.
