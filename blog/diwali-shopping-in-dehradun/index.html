Diwali Shopping in Dehradun: Markets, Sweets and Gifts

Which bazaar to go to for what, the festive traffic rules around Paltan Bazaar, and gifts that come from Doon rather than a mall.

Diwali shopping in Dehradun mostly happens in one small corner of the city. Paltan Bazaar, Moti Bazaar, Hanuman Chowk and Dhamawala sit within walking distance of the Clock Tower, and in the week before the festival it can feel like half the valley is in those lanes at the same time.

This guide covers where to go for what, how the festive traffic rules work this year, and what to buy if you want gifts that are actually from here.

Diwali 2026 dates

The five days fall like this in 2026:

  • Dhanteras: Friday, 6 November
  • Choti Diwali: Saturday, 7 November
  • Diwali and Lakshmi Puja: Sunday, 8 November
  • Govardhan Puja: Monday, 9 November
  • Bhai Dooj: Tuesday, 10 November

Diwali is on a Sunday this year, and that matters here. Most shops in Paltan Bazaar normally stay shut on Sundays. Some will open for the festival, but don't count on finding your usual shop open on the day. Plan to finish your shopping by Saturday evening.

Paltan Bazaar traffic rules this festive season

The district administration has made Paltan Bazaar vehicle-free from 11 am to 7 pm for the festive season. According to the city's traders' association, the rule covers Moti Bazaar and Hanuman Chowk too, and it started on 27 October. Loading vehicles, three-wheelers and cars are stopped at the entry points. Two-wheelers are allowed. Shopkeepers can load and unload only before 11 am and after 7 pm.

In practice this makes the bazaar much easier on foot. It also means you can't drive up to a shop, load a carton of diyas and leave. Park outside and carry what you buy, or ask the shop to keep your bags until the evening.

For last year's Diwali, the traffic police listed Gandhi Inter College and CNI School as parking for Paltan Bazaar shoppers, along with the MDDA complex at Ghanta Ghar and the multi-storey parking at Kanak Chowk. This year's list may differ, so check the Dehradun Police announcements before you set out.

Local tip · go before 11 am on a weekday. The lanes are quieter, and shopkeepers have time to show you more than the first three options.

Where to go for Diwali shopping in Dehradun

Paltan Bazaar: clothes, dry fruits and the main festive buying

Paltan Bazaar starts at the Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower) and is the oldest market in the city. For Diwali it has most of what a household needs: new clothes, dry fruits, spices, Doon basmati, gift boxes and decorations. It's also the most crowded place in Dehradun from Dhanteras onwards, so go with a list.

Dhamawala: utensils and jewellery for Dhanteras

Dhanteras is the day many families buy something in metal: a steel or brass utensil, a silver coin, a piece of jewellery. The Dhamawala section, towards the railway station end of the bazaar, is where Dehradun goes for utensils and jewellery. Expect it to be packed on 6 November.

Moti Bazaar and Hanuman Chowk

Moti Bazaar has wholesale-style shops, which helps if you're buying the same gift for a lot of people. It also has the city's best-known chole katlamba, so plan your food break here (our street food guide has the details). Near the Hanuman temple at Hanuman Chowk, Chetan Pooriwala serves puri sabzi, which is a good reason to start a shopping morning there.

Indira Market

Near Darshan Lal Chowk, Indira Market is the place for everyday clothes, shoes and bags at reasonable prices. It's a calmer option if you need a new kurta for Lakshmi Puja and don't want to fight the Paltan Bazaar crowd.

Tibetan Market, Parade Ground

Diwali falls right when the winter stock arrives. The stalls at the Tibetan Market, Parade Ground sell jackets, sweaters, caps and gloves, and a good jacket makes a practical Diwali gift for anyone heading into a Doon winter.

Gifts that come from Dehradun

Dry fruit boxes and chocolate hampers are available everywhere. If you want something that says Doon, try these instead.

  • A box of rusks or cake rusk from an old bakery. Ellora’s Melting Moments on Rajpur Road is the famous name, and there's Quality Bakery near the Clock Tower. Our bakeries guide has more.
  • Doon basmati. Paltan Bazaar is where to buy it. A small bag of good basmati is a gift most elders will appreciate.
  • Pahadi produce. Honey, gahat, rajma, mandua flour and pahadi salt. Pahadi Store, Dehradun has branches in Ballupur and on the Doon University campus, and PAHADI ORGANIC HUB is in Ajabpur Khurd. A basket of three or four of these makes a better hamper than most ready-made ones.
  • Handloom. Himalayan Weavers Dehradun in Old Rajpur sells handloom shawls and stoles. The older woollen shops in Paltan Bazaar, like Urvashi Wool House and Bareilly Wool Bhandar, are worth a look for shawls too.
  • A book. The English Book Depot is one of the city's best-known bookshops, and Moti Bazaar has several book depots.

Sweets

Every part of Dehradun has its own halwai, and most families already have a favourite. Two things help in Diwali week. Place big orders for gift boxes a few days ahead, because the popular shops are busiest on Choti Diwali and on the day itself. And buy fresh milk sweets close to when you'll give them, since they don't keep long. Kaju katli and dry sweets travel better if you're sending a box out of town.

A simple Diwali shopping plan

Start at Hanuman Chowk around 10 am with puri sabzi. Walk up through Moti Bazaar and Paltan Bazaar for clothes, dry fruits and decorations while the vehicle ban keeps the lanes clear. Break for chole katlamba. Do Dhamawala last if you're buying utensils or jewellery, then finish at a bakery near the Clock Tower for rusks to take home. Keep the Tibetan Market for a separate evening.

Tips before you go

  • Carry a cloth bag or two. You'll be walking with everything you buy.
  • Most shops take UPI. Keep some cash for small stalls.
  • Bargaining is normal in Paltan Bazaar, Indira Market and the Tibetan Market. It isn't in branded showrooms.
  • Keep your phone and wallet in a front pocket in crowded lanes.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best market for Diwali shopping in Dehradun?

Paltan Bazaar, starting at the Clock Tower. With Moti Bazaar, Hanuman Chowk and Dhamawala next to it, you can buy clothes, dry fruits, decorations, utensils and jewellery on one walk.

Can I drive into Paltan Bazaar during Diwali 2026?

Not between 11 am and 7 pm. The festive rule keeps cars, three-wheelers and loading vehicles out of Paltan Bazaar during those hours. Two-wheelers are allowed, according to the traders' association.

When is Dhanteras in 2026?

Friday, 6 November. Diwali and Lakshmi Puja are on Sunday, 8 November.

Where can I buy utensils and jewellery for Dhanteras in Dehradun?

The Dhamawala section of Paltan Bazaar, near the railway station end.

What gifts can I buy in Dehradun that are local?

Bakery rusks, Doon basmati, pahadi honey and pulses, and handloom shawls are the most Dehradun gifts you can give.
