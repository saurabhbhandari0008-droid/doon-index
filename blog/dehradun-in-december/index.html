Dehradun in December: Weather, What to Wear and What to Do

Sunny afternoons, cold nights and very little rain. The IMD's own numbers for a December visit, what they mean for packing, and how to plan the days.

Dehradun in December is dry, sunny in the afternoon and properly cold after dark. If you've only seen the city in the monsoon, it can feel like a different place. The hills are sharp against the sky, the roads are dry, and by 6 pm everyone has a shawl on.

This guide uses the India Meteorological Department's long-term figures for the city, so you know what to pack, and then covers how to spend the days.

Dehradun weather in December

IMD's 1991 to 2020 averages for Dehradun put the December daytime high at 21.7°C and the night-time low at 7.6°C. It cools as the month goes on. IMD's older climate report has the normal high falling from about 23°C in the first week to about 19°C by the end of the month, with the low going from about 8°C to about 6°C.

December is one of the driest months of the year. The 1991 to 2020 average is 13 mm of rain, spread over about one rainy day. In most years you won't need an umbrella at all.

It has been much colder than the averages suggest. The city's lowest recorded winter temperature, 0.0°C, came on 31 December 1954, and on 31 December 2002 the high reached only 8.4°C.

Fog and frost

IMD describes Doon winters as having mist, fog and sometimes frost in the mornings, followed by bright sunny days. Fog is less common than it is in the plains: it forms at night and in the early morning, and it rarely lasts until noon. If you're driving in from Delhi or Saharanpur, the fog on the plains is usually worse than anything you'll meet in Dehradun itself.

Short days

The sun rises around 7 am and sets around 5:20 pm. The temperature drops fast once it goes behind the hills. Plan outdoor things for 10 am to 3 pm and keep the evenings for food and markets.

What to wear in Dehradun in December

The gap between afternoon and night is about 14 degrees, so pack layers rather than one heavy coat.

  • Daytime: a full-sleeve shirt or light sweater. In the sun at 1 pm you may take even that off.
  • Evening: a proper jacket or a thick sweater, plus a shawl or muffler. Locals switch to woollens as soon as the sun sets.
  • Night and early morning: thermals if you're staying out past 9 pm or leaving early for Mussoorie.
  • Feet: closed shoes and woollen socks. Most homes and many guesthouses don't have central heating, so floors get cold.
  • If you go up to Mussoorie, Dhanaulti or Chakrata, add gloves and a cap. It is several degrees colder up there.

Local tip · if you forget something, Paltan Bazaar has several wool shops, Urvashi Wool House and Bareilly Wool Bhandar among them, for shawls, mufflers, caps and gloves.

Things to do in Dehradun in December

Watch the winter birds at Asan

Asan Conservation Reserve, at the Asan Barrage on the Dehradun–Paonta road, is Uttarakhand's first Ramsar site. Migratory ducks such as the ruddy shelduck arrive every winter, and the best months are November to March. Go early in the morning and take binoculars if you have them.

Spend the afternoon outdoors

December afternoons are good for the walks that are too hot in May. The Forest Research Institute has two visitor sessions a day, 10 am to 1:30 pm and 1:30 to 5 pm, so book the morning one if you want the light on the main building. Doon Dekho's FRI guide has the 2026 fees.

Anand Van in Jhajra, Gandhi Park on Rajpur Road and the zoo at Malsi are all easier in cool weather too. With kids, see the guide to places to visit in Dehradun with kids.

Skip the water, or go knowing it's cold

Robber's Cave and Sahastradhara are both about walking through or sitting in water. In December that water is cold, and the stream at Robber's Cave is in shade for much of the day. You can still go and walk the cave or see the dripping rocks at Sahastradhara. Just don't plan a long paddle, and bring dry socks.

Eat for the weather

Winter is when a plate of jhol momo makes most sense: momos sitting in a thin, sour, spicy soup. The best momos in Dehradun guide lists ten places by area. For something sweet, Ellora's Melting Moments on Rajpur Road is known for its plum cake, which the older bakeries make for Christmas. Doon Dekho's guide to Dehradun's bakeries has more.

Christmas and New Year

Doon Dekho will have separate guides to Christmas and New Year's Eve in Dehradun later this season, with venues. If you're booking a dinner for either night, book early and confirm the price when you do.

Go up to Mussoorie for the Winterline Carnival

Mussoorie holds its Winterline Carnival at the end of December, with folk performances on Mall Road, evening concerts and stalls selling pahadi food. In 2024 it ran for five days from 26 December. In 2025 it started on 24 December and ran for six days. The 2026 dates hadn't been announced when this was written, so check the Mussoorie or Dehradun administration's notices closer to the time. The road up gets crowded during the carnival, so leave early.

Will it snow?

Don't expect it in the city. Dehradun sits at about 640 m, and even its record low only touched freezing. Snow falls higher up, in places such as Dhanaulti, Chakrata and sometimes Mussoorie, but it depends on western disturbances and isn't guaranteed in December. If snow is the reason for your trip, watch the forecast and keep your dates flexible. Doon Dekho's guide to snowfall near Dehradun is coming next week.

Is December a good time to visit Dehradun?

If you like dry weather and clear views, yes. The roads are dry, the hills are visible, and the cafés and markets are busy without the summer crowds. Hotels in Mussoorie fill up between Christmas and New Year, which means Dehradun gets busier too, so book early if you're coming in the last week.

The main downside is the short day. You get about ten hours of useful daylight, and once the sun goes, most people head indoors.

What is the weather like in Dehradun in December?

Dry and sunny in the day, cold at night. IMD's 1991 to 2020 averages are a high of 21.7°C and a low of 7.6°C, with about 13 mm of rain in the month.

Does it snow in Dehradun in December?

Don't expect it in the city: Dehradun's lowest recorded winter temperature is 0.0°C (31 December 1954). Snow falls higher up, in Dhanaulti, Chakrata and sometimes Mussoorie, and isn't guaranteed in December.

What should I wear in Dehradun in December?

Layers. A light sweater in the afternoon, a jacket and shawl or muffler after sunset, and thermals for late nights or early starts. Add gloves and a cap for Mussoorie or Dhanaulti.

Is there fog in Dehradun in December?

Some mornings, but less than in the plains. IMD says winter fog in Dehradun forms at night and early morning and rarely lasts until noon.

When is the Mussoorie Winterline Carnival?

At the end of December. It began on 26 December in 2024 and on 24 December in 2025. The 2026 dates hadn't been announced as of November 2026.
