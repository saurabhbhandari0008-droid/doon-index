Mall of Dehradun: Stores, PVR, Food and How to Get There

Uttarakhand's largest mall, on Haridwar Road. Which brands are there, the PVR INOX multiplex, Timezone, the food court, the Lemon Tree hotel and how to reach it.

Mall of Dehradun opened on 1 June 2024 on Haridwar Road, at Mohkampur Khurd on the southern side of the city. Pacific Group, which also runs Pacific Mall on Rajpur Road, calls it the largest shopping destination in Uttarakhand, with over 10 lakh square feet of built-up space. It's aimed at shoppers from Haridwar, Rishikesh, Roorkee and Saharanpur as well as Dehradun.

Stores and brands

These are brands named in Pacific Group's launch announcement and on the brands' own store pages. Stores open and close, so check a brand's store locator before making a special trip.

  • Department and fashion stores: Lifestyle, Westside, Pantaloons and Homecentre.
  • Premium and international brands: Lacoste, Guess, Gant and Onitsuka Tiger.
  • Beauty: Nykaa Luxe.
  • Electronics: Croma.

Cinema and entertainment

The PVR INOX multiplex is on the second floor. Timezone, the arcade chain, has an outlet here too, which makes the mall an easy outing with children.

Food

The food court gives space to Uttarakhand food, which is unusual for a mall food court.

Hotel on site

There's a Lemon Tree hotel on the same premises. It's useful if you're coming from out of town to shop or catch a film and want to stay the night.

How to get there

The mall is on Haridwar Road, on the way out of the city towards Doiwala and Rishikesh. From the Clock Tower, take Haridwar Road south. If you're coming from Rajpur Road or the Mussoorie side, leave extra time for the drive across town.

For anyone living in Doiwala, Rishikesh Road or the Haridwar Road colonies, it's the closest big mall. If you live on the north side of the city, Pacific Mall on Rajpur Road is closer and has many of the same kinds of stores.

Mall of Dehradun vs Pacific Mall

Mall of Dehradun is newer and much bigger, with more premium international brands, Croma and the Lemon Tree hotel. Pacific Mall is closer for most people in the city, and it has Shoppers Stop and Lifestyle. Both have PVR cinemas.
