Snowfall Near Dehradun: Where and When to See Snow

The nearest places to Dehradun that get snow, how far each one is, which months to go and what to check before you drive up.

Dehradun itself doesn't get snow. The valley is too low, and winter here means foggy mornings and sunny afternoons. But within two hours of the city there are hills that do get snow most winters, and every December a lot of us start checking the weather for Dhanaulti and Chakrata.

These are the four places people from Doon usually drive to, with distances from Dehradun.

Dhanaulti, about 59 km

Dhanaulti sits at about 2,286 m on the Mussoorie–Chamba road, 24 km past Mussoorie. It's the most common snow trip from Dehradun because it's close. The Amber and Dhara eco parks are about 200 m apart and are pleasant to walk through when they're white. Surkanda Devi temple is close by if you want to add a climb. Winter temperatures run from about 7°C down to -1°C.

Chakrata, about 89 km

Chakrata is an Army cantonment town north-west of Dehradun, reached through Kalsi. It's further and the road is slower, but it's quieter than the Mussoorie side. News reports from past winters mention snowfall around Chakrata in December. Tiger Falls, Deoban and the Budher caves are the usual sights. It is a cantonment, so carry ID and follow the signboards.

Kanatal, about 78 km

Kanatal is on the Chamba–Mussoorie road, 38 km beyond Mussoorie and 18 km from Chamba. It's mostly known for camping. Heavy snowfall in winter often blocks the roads here, which is worth knowing before you plan a day trip. Winter temperatures range from about -5°C to 10°C.

Mussoorie, about 32 km

Mussoorie is the closest and the easiest, but it gets less snow than Dhanaulti and it doesn't come every year. Landour, above the town, has the better chance. If snow is forecast, Mussoorie fills up fast, so go on a weekday if you can.

When does it snow near Dehradun?

Most snow falls between late December and February. Dates change every year and depend on western disturbances, so there is no fixed week to book. Watch the IMD forecast and local news from mid-December. Winter rain in Dehradun usually comes with snow in the higher hills.

Before you drive up

After a fresh snowfall, roads to Dhanaulti and Kanatal can close for hours. Police sometimes stop vehicles until the road is cleared.

Leave early. Traffic piles up on the Mussoorie side on snow weekends, and you want to be coming back down before dark when the road freezes.

Carry warm layers, gloves and a spare pair of socks. Your shoes will get wet.

If you're not used to driving on snow, park where the snow starts and walk, or hire a local taxi.

Fill up with fuel in Dehradun before you start.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the nearest place to Dehradun for snowfall?

Mussoorie is the closest at about 32 km, but Dhanaulti, about 59 km away, gets snow more reliably.

When is the best time to see snow near Dehradun?

Late December to February. Check the forecast and local news before you go, because snowfall dates change every year.

Does Dehradun get snow?

No. The city is in the valley and doesn't get snow. The nearest snow is in the hills above Mussoorie.

Is it safe to drive to Dhanaulti in snow?

The road can close after heavy snow, and it freezes after dark. Go early, come down before sunset, and don't push on if police have stopped traffic.
