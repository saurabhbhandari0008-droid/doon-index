New Year Party in Dehradun 2027: Venues, Prices and Getting Home

Tickets for 31 December 2026 aren't out yet. Here's what last year's parties cost, where they were held, and how to plan the night so you get home.

A New Year party in Dehradun usually means one of three things: a ticketed night at a lounge or club in the city, a dinner-and-bonfire package at a resort on the edge of town, or a hotel stay with the party included. When we checked on 23 November 2026, no venue had put 31 December 2026 tickets on sale on district.in, the ticketing site where most Dehradun events are listed. Last year's round-ups of New Year parties came out in early and mid-December.

So this guide uses last year's parties as a guide to what to expect. Every price below is from listings for 31 December 2025, published by the city sites Dehradun Wants (17 December 2025) and WanderOn (9 December 2025). Prices for this year will be different. We'll add 2026 events when they're announced.

New Year party in Dehradun: what last year's cost

Resort parties

The Needam Resort, Maldevta. The New Year Eve Fiesta ran from 9 pm on 31 December to 1 am. Entry was ₹2,999 and included a gala dinner, snacks, non-alcoholic drinks, a DJ and live music, cake cutting, games and a bonfire. Maldevta is out past Raipur, so you'll need a ride back arranged before you go.

Vishram Resort. Its party, Naya Daur 2.0, started at ₹2,200 and allowed couple and family entries. The listing mentioned a two-hour unlimited buffet, welcome drinks, a bonfire and fireworks.

The Shahanshahi Resort, Old Mussoorie Road. It sold a stay-and-dine package with a gala dinner, bar, meals and a cultural programme. The listing didn't give a price.

Clubs and lounges in the city

The Phoenix Club, Suddowala. On the second floor of Sai Tower. Its New Year's Eve party started at 6 pm on 31 December 2025, with tickets from ₹1,449.

Ministry of Sound, Centrio Mall. On the sixth floor of Centrio Mall, on New Cantonment Road. Last year it held a Sufi night from 8 pm, by reservation only.

Rumours, Rajpur Road. On the fifth floor of the Euphoria complex at 158 Rajpur Road, Malsi. It advertised a New Year's Eve bash but didn't publish a price.

Dehradun Social, Hathibarkala Salwala. Its party was sold on district.in, ran for four and a half hours, and was for guests aged 21 and over only, with no children allowed. Check the age rule on any ticket before you book for a mixed group.

Some lounges were listed with their normal prices rather than a New Year ticket: QB Lounge in Axion Tower near Pacific Mall at about ₹550 a plate, Opal Lounge in the International Trade Centre on Rajpur Road at ₹1,600 for two, and Bristal Bar on Kaulagarh Road at ₹1,000 for two without alcohol. Ask whether those change on 31 December.

Hotel stays

If you want to stay where the party is, WanderOn listed room rates for last New Year of ₹17,000 to ₹28,000 a night at the Hyatt Regency Dehradun and ₹7,000 to ₹13,000 at Tree of Life Dehradun Foothills Resort. Those were room prices, not party tickets, and hotels set their New Year packages separately.

When to book

Watch district.in and the venues' own Instagram pages from the start of December. Last year the city round-ups were published on 9 and 17 December, which tells you roughly when most venues announced.

Before you pay, ask four things: what the ticket includes (food, drinks, both), whether it's per person or per couple, the age rule, and what time the bar closes. Dehradun bars are normally expected to close at 11 pm. Whether a venue has permission to run later on 31 December is decided each year, so ask the venue, not a reseller.

Traffic on 31 December

Dehradun Police put out a traffic plan for 30 and 31 December last year. It had 22 checking points, including Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower), Kirsali Chowk, the crossing on Sahastradhara Road, Mussoorie Diversion and Kuthal Gate. Police said they had zero tolerance for drunk driving, rash driving and speeding. Traffic heading to Rajpur Road was diverted at points near Pacific Mall, via Sai Mandir and IT Park.

If you're driving into town for a party, the 2025 plan listed public parking at Rangers Ground near Buddha Chowk (300 cars), the MDDA parking near Ghanta Ghar (300), the Rajiv Gandhi Complex near Tehsil Chowk (400) and the Parade Ground near the Tibetan Market (100).

If you're going up to Mussoorie

The road to Mussoorie is at its busiest on New Year's Eve. Last year's plan sent cars through Kuthal Gate and, once Mussoorie's parking was 70% full, to King Craig, with local taxis or shuttles onward. When King Craig filled up, cars were stopped at Gajji Band and people took taxis from there. Our Christmas and New Year guide has more on Mussoorie and the Winterline Carnival. Book your stay before you drive up, and don't plan to come back down late.

Local tip · Save 112 in your phone. It's the 24-hour emergency number in Uttarakhand. Women can also use the Gaura Shakti section of the Uttarakhand Police app, which has an SOS button.

Getting home

Decide how you're getting home before you leave. If you're drinking, don't drive: there are checking points across the city that night. Book a cab in advance rather than counting on finding one after midnight, or pick a party close to where you're staying. The resort parties at Maldevta and on Old Mussoorie Road are a long way out at 1 am.

For a quieter night, an early dinner at a café or restaurant and home before the roads fill is still the easiest plan. Our guide to things to do in Dehradun at night lists the places that stay open late.

Where can I find New Year party tickets in Dehradun?

district.in carries most ticketed events in Dehradun, and venues post their own on Instagram. Last year most listings appeared in the first half of December.

How much does a New Year party in Dehradun cost?

Last year, tickets started from ₹1,449 at The Phoenix Club, and resort parties with dinner were ₹2,200 to ₹2,999, at Vishram Resort and The Needam Resort. Prices for 2026 will differ.

Are there family-friendly New Year parties in Dehradun?

Yes. Vishram Resort allowed family entries last year, and resort parties with a buffet and bonfire usually suit families better than lounges. Some club nights are 21 and over only, so check the ticket.

Is there a traffic plan for New Year's Eve in Dehradun?

Dehradun Police issued one for 30 and 31 December 2025, with diversions, 22 checking points and parking for Mussoorie traffic. Look for this year's plan in the last week of December.

What time do parties end in Dehradun on New Year's Eve?

It depends on the venue's permission for the night. Bars are normally expected to close at 11 pm. Last year The Needam Resort's party was listed until 1 am. Ask the venue before you book.
