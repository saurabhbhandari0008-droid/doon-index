Things to Do in Dehradun at Night

Dehradun is a city that eats early and goes home. What's still open after dark, what closes before you'd think, and how to plan an evening.

Things to do in Dehradun at night come down to a short list: walk an evening market, eat, watch a film at a mall, or sit in a café or bar on Rajpur Road until closing. This isn't a late city. Markets and malls are listed as closing around 10 pm, bars are expected to close at 11 pm, and in winter it's dark before 6. Knowing that in advance makes for a better evening than finding a shutter down at 9:30.

Opening hours in this guide come from listings and city guides, and they often disagree with each other. Use them to plan, then check with the place before you set out.

When it gets dark

In mid-November the sun sets in Dehradun at about 5:20 pm and it's fully dark by about 5:45. By the end of November sunset is a few minutes earlier. Nights are cold from December: IMD's long-term averages put the mean minimum for December at about 7°C, and the district's own figures go as low as 4°C. Carry a jacket even if the afternoon was warm.

Evening markets

Ghanta Ghar (Clock Tower) is lit up at night and is the natural place to start. Paltan Bazaar runs down from it and stays open into the evening. Listings give closing times anywhere from 10 to 11 pm, so aim to be there by 7 or 8 if you want to shop rather than just walk.

Indira Market, at Darshan Lal Chowk, is busiest in the evening, according to the TripXL guide, which lists it as open until about 9:15 pm. It's a good place to eat cheaply after Paltan.

Films and malls

The malls are the easiest late-evening plan, especially with children or in bad weather. Pacific Mall on Rajpur Road and Centrio Mall both have PVR cinemas, and guides list the malls as open from about 10 or 11 am to 10 pm. Funky Island, the bowling and games area inside Pacific Mall, is listed as open until 11 pm. Check the PVR app for show timings if you want a late film.

Things to do in Dehradun at night: cafés and bars

Rajpur Road is where most people go after dark. The cafés cluster around Kishanpur and further up towards Rajpur, and several of the bars and lounges in city guides are on the same road. Our Rajpur Road café guide has the details on which places suit a group, a date or a quiet coffee.

For bars, plan around 11 pm. In November 2024 the Chief Minister told the Dehradun district administration to act against bars and pubs operating after 11 pm, with penalties up to cancelling the licence, and enforcement of closing times came up again in 2026. Some guides still list bars as open until midnight. Don't build a plan on that.

Eating late

Plan dinner earlier than you would in a big city, because several well-known places are listed as closing before 10. Two examples: Kumar Sweets & Restaurant on Sahastradhara Road is listed until about 9:45 pm, and Tibet Kitchen (Didi Momo's) near Gandhi Park on Rajpur Road until 8:30 pm. On Rajpur Road and in the old town, a few cafés and family restaurants are listed until 11 pm. If you're coming back from a day trip at 10, order in or eat on the way.

What isn't open at night

Several places people ask about close early, so don't save them for the evening:

  • Gandhi Park, next to the Clock Tower: open 5 to 9 am and 4 to 6:30 pm.
  • Robbers Cave (Guchhupani): listed as open 7 am to 6 pm.
  • Sahastradhara and Maldevta: go in daylight. Both rivers rise quickly after rain in the hills.
  • Bakeries such as Ellora's Melting Moments on Rajpur Road: listed as closing at 5 pm.

Winter nights: the Winterline Carnival

If you're here at the end of December, the Winterline Carnival in Mussoorie is the main evening event nearby. The Ministry of Culture describes it as an annual festival from about 25 to 29 or 30 December, with campfire nights, music and a food festival. The 2025 edition ran from 24 to 29 December. Dates for 2026 hadn't been announced when we wrote this. The drive back down is in the dark, so either stay the night in Mussoorie or have someone else drive.

Local tip · save 112 in your phone before you go out. It's the 24-hour emergency number in Uttarakhand, and Dehradun Police have told women stuck without transport at night to call it.

Getting home safely

Book a cab or auto before the place you're in closes, not after. Women can also use the Gaura Shakti section of the Uttarakhand Police app, which gives direct contact with a designated woman police officer and an SOS button. If you drive out of the city at night, towards Mussoorie for example, take the hill road slowly.

Is there nightlife in Dehradun?

A small amount. Most of it is cafés, lounges and bars on Rajpur Road. Bars are expected to close at 11 pm, and most shops close by about 10 pm.

What time do bars close in Dehradun?

11 pm. The state government has ordered action against bars and pubs that stay open later, so don't rely on older listings that say midnight.

What can I do in Dehradun in the evening with family?

Walk Paltan Bazaar and see the Clock Tower lit up, eat at Indira Market, or watch a film at Pacific Mall or Centrio Mall. Gandhi Park is open until 6:30 pm.

Is Dehradun safe at night?

Stick to busy areas such as Rajpur Road and the old town, arrange your ride home before places close, and call 112 in an emergency. Women can use the Gaura Shakti feature in the Uttarakhand Police app.

What time does it get dark in Dehradun in winter?

Around 5:45 pm in mid to late November, a few minutes after sunset at about 5:20 pm.
